# Bio
Luc is an Applied Physics PhD student at Cornell University offering tutoring services for a wide range of courses. He holds a master's degree in computer science, and bachelor's degrees in physics, mathematics, and computer science, cum laude, from UMass Amherst. He has extensive teaching experience, from the high-school level as a temporary math instructor at the Hotchkiss School, to co-teaching undergraduate-level introductory physics and graduate-level quantum information courses.

Rates generally vary depending on the course, meeting time, financial need, and other factors. The first meeting is at a reduced price to see if I'm a good fit for you. I generally tutor over Zoom, but in-person can be arranged in Ithaca, NY.

For any questions, I can be reached at lab392+tutoring@cornell.edu. I do my best to respond both to emails and requests to the above form within two business days!
