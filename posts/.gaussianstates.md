# State Vector Flaws

Let's consider a fairly practical state that you could expect to come up quite often - one in thermal equilibrium. This state, called a thermal state, has a density matrix of the form
$$\rho_\text{th} = \frac{e^{-\beta \hat{H}}}{ \text{Tr} \left[  e^{-\beta \hat{H}} \right]} $$

Or, in the Fock basis, this can be written as
$$\rho^\text{th} (\bar{n}) = \sum_{n=0}^{+\infty} \frac{\bar{n}^n}{(\bar{n}+1)^{n+1}} | n \rangle \langle n |    $$

This can be formulated in terms of temperature, but here its a function of $\bar{n}$, the average number of particles found in the system. The most important thing to note here is that, other than $\bar{n} = 0$, this has a non-zero term for all states in the Fock basis.
